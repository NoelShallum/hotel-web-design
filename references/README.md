## Notes on reference 1

What it shows: people sitting around two log-ringed campfires in a tall pine forest, with tents in the background, at dusk with smoke drifting through the trees.

Style qualities to carry over:
- **Mood:** warm, communal, grounded. Gathering around a fire suggests community and going back to the roots.
- **Palette:** deep forest greens and earthy browns, lit by glowing amber and orange from the fire.
- **Light:** low, moody, high contrast. Dark edges with the warm glow at the centre, and haze or smoke for atmosphere.
- **Composition:** wide shot looking up through tall tree trunks, with people at the edges and the fire as the focal point.
- **Texture:** natural materials such as wood, canvas, stone and ash.

Caveats:
- The image looks AI-generated and is a phone screenshot, so it is only a mood reference.
- v1 of the site uses two crops of it (phone clock and buttons removed) as placeholder imagery, marked as such on the page. They live in `site/assets/img/`.
- The site should use real photography of the property once available.
