## 3. Colour

### 3.1 The palette: Sandhya (संध्या)

Sandhya means twilight, and is also the name of the dawn and dusk prayers. Deep purples from the night sky, two oranges from the last of the sun, ivory and black to hold it together.

| Swatch | Name | Hindi | Hex | Role |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ■ | Dark Amethyst | जमुनिया | `#240046` | 60% Trust: logo, hero, headings, footers |
| ■ | Indigo Velvet | नील | `#5A189A` | 60% Trust, secondary: second block colour, hover states |
| ■ | Royal Violet | बैंगनी | `#7B2CBF` | 60% Trust, tertiary: links and icons on ivory, Devanagari headings on ivory |
| ■ | Ivory white | श्वेत | `#FFFFF0` | 30% Structure: backgrounds, cards, readable surfaces |
| ■ | Pumpkin Spice | नारंगी | `#FF6D00` | 10% Action: the main button, one promotional block |
| ■ | Amber Glow | सुनहरा | `#FF9E00` | 10% Action, highlight: tags, prices, Devanagari on dark |
| ■ | Black | काला | `#000000` | Text, outlines, hard shadows |

### 3.2 The 60/30/10 split

- **60% Trust:** the three purples. They carry the logo, headings, hero and footers, and the Kath-Kuni stripe.
- **30% Structure:** ivory. It sets the reading surfaces so the purple never feels heavy.
- **10% Action:** the two oranges. Buttons, tags and one callout block per page.

The split is counted over the whole page, not per section. A page may be mostly ivory in the middle and mostly purple at the top and bottom.

### 3.3 Colour psychology (why these colours)

- **Purples:** spirituality, introspection, creativity, long linked to royalty. Dark amethyst reads as night sky and depth, which suits sound healing and quiet. In the common yoga colour system, indigo is the third eye and violet the crown.
- **Ivory:** a warm soft white. It is gentler than pure white and lets the type breathe.
- **Oranges:** warmth, energy, optimism, appetite. In the same yoga system orange is the sacral colour of creativity and joy, a fit for the workshops and café. Purple and orange are near-opposites, so a button jumps off the page, and together they read as dusk over the mountains.
- **Note:** colour-psychology claims come from design writing, not peer-reviewed research. Treat them as reasoning, not proof.

### 3.4 Rules of use

1. **One Pumpkin button per screen.** Secondary buttons use ivory, Amethyst or Black.
2. **Text on either orange is always Black.** Never ivory on orange.
3. **Never orange text on ivory** (Pumpkin 2.8:1, Amber 2.1:1). Orange is for fills, outlines and shadows there.
4. **Amber is for highlights on the dark purples** and for tags.
5. **Violet is for links, icons and headings on ivory.** Never for text on Amethyst (2.5:1) and never for Black text on Violet (3.0:1).
6. **Black text only on ivory, Pumpkin or Amber.** Ivory text on Amethyst, Indigo or Violet.
7. **Every block has a 3px outline and a hard offset shadow, with no blur.** On dark purple surfaces the outline and shadow flip to ivory or Amber, since Black disappears into them.
8. **Kath-Kuni stripe** alternates Indigo and Violet bands with Black "mortar" lines. It is the only texture on the site.

### 3.5 Approved text and background pairs

| Text | On | Contrast | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Black | Ivory | 20.8 | Body text |
| Amethyst | Ivory | 17.9 | Headings, wordmark |
| Indigo | Ivory | 10.2 | Handwritten notes, subheads |
| Violet | Ivory | 7.0 | Devanagari headings, links |
| Ivory | Amethyst | 17.9 | Body and headings on dark |
| Ivory | Indigo | 10.2 | Bands, cards |
| Ivory | Violet | 7.0 | Large text and tags only |
| Amber | Amethyst | 8.7 | Devanagari headings, highlights |
| Amber | Indigo | 5.0 | Large text, ✦ separators |
| Pumpkin | Amethyst | 6.4 | Large text, graphics |
| Black | Pumpkin | 7.4 | Buttons |
| Black | Amber | 10.1 | Tags, highlights |

**Do not use:** Pumpkin on ivory (2.8), Amber on ivory (2.1), Ivory on Pumpkin (2.8), Violet on Amethyst (2.5), Black on Violet (3.0), Black on Indigo (2.0), Pumpkin on Indigo (3.7).

### 3.6 Caution

Both oranges sit close to saffron, which carries political associations in India today. Next to purple they read as sunset. Keep the oranges as accents, and avoid pairing them with green or white flags or tricolour arrangements.

## 4. Typography

The type system is **Signboard**: painted-sign weight, with Devanagari at the same strength as Latin. All faces are free on Google Fonts.

| Role | Face | Weights | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display | **Eczar** | 800 (600 for small subheads) | Headlines, wordmark, buttons, Devanagari |
| Body | **Hind** | 400, 600, 700 | Paragraphs, navigation, forms, labels |
| Note | **Kalam** | 400, 700 | Short handwritten asides and captions only |

Fallback stacks: Eczar → `Georgia, serif`; Hind → `system-ui, sans-serif`; Kalam → `cursive`.

### 4.1 Scale

| Style | Size | Line height | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Display (h1) | 52 to 112px, fluid | 0.98 | Eczar 800 |
| Devanagari display | 32 to 64px, fluid | 1.3 | Eczar 800. Taller line height so the headline strokes are not clipped |
| h2 | 36 to 60px, fluid | 1.1 | Eczar 800 |
| h3 | 24 to 30px | 1.25 | Eczar 800 |
| Body | 18px | 1.55 | Hind 400, 65 characters per line at most |
| Label | 13px, uppercase, 0.12em tracking | 1.3 | Hind 700 |
| Note | 22 to 24px | 1.3 | Kalam, never for more than one line or two |

### 4.2 Devanagari rules

- **Always pair Devanagari with English.** The Devanagari line is a headline, the English line is the meaning. Never Devanagari alone.
- Devanagari is set at the same size as or larger than the English next to it, in Eczar 800.
- Mark Devanagari with `lang="hi"` so screen readers and browsers pick the right shaping.
- **Have every Devanagari phrase checked by a native Hindi speaker before launch.** The drafts in this repo were written by Claude and are not yet reviewed.

## 5. Layout and components

### 5.1 Principles

1. **Heavy and clear.** Big type, big blocks, nothing delicate.
2. **Outlined and shadowed.** 3px Black outline, hard offset shadow (4, 6 or 10px), zero blur, zero radius.
3. **Asymmetric where it helps.** Blocks may sit off-centre, but the reading order stays simple.
4. **One texture.** The Kath-Kuni stripe. Nothing else decorative except the dot row below.

### 5.2 Components

| Component | Spec |
|---|---|
| **Primary button** | Pumpkin fill, Black text, Eczar 800 20px, 3px Black outline, 6px Black shadow. Minimum height 52px. Hover: lifts 2px up-left and the shadow grows to 8px. Press: moves into the shadow and the shadow drops to 0 |
| **Secondary button** | Ivory fill, Black text and outline, same size. On dark surfaces: transparent fill, ivory text and outline |
| **Card** | Ivory fill, 3px Black outline, 6px Black shadow, 22 to 28px padding. One featured card per row may be Amethyst with an Amber Devanagari title and a Pumpkin shadow |
| **Tag** | Amber fill, Black text, 3px outline, uppercase label style |
| **Placeholder chip [TBC]** | Dashed 3px Black outline on Amber, Black text. Marks content that still needs the founder's confirmation. Search the code for `class="tbc"` before launch and resolve every one |
| **Navigation** | Ivory bar with a 3px Black bottom edge. Wordmark left, links right. The bar button is Amethyst (the Pumpkin button belongs to the page) |
| **Form field** | Ivory fill, 3px Black outline, 52px high, label above in the label style. Focus: 3px Violet outline with a 3px offset |
| **Kath-Kuni stripe** | Repeating bands: Indigo 46px, Black 6px, Violet 46px, Black 6px. 18px high as a horizontal divider, 3px Black edges |
| **Dot row** | Amber dots (12px) on Amethyst, 26px grid, 30px high. Used once, above the footer |

### 5.3 Motion

- Buttons and cards move on hover and press only. No scroll animation, no parallax.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`: remove all transitions.

## 6. Imagery

**Reference:** [references/images/campfire-forest-gathering.jpg](./images/campfire-forest-gathering.jpg).

**Direction**
- Warm, communal, moody. People gathered, a fire or a lamp as the focal point, tall trees around.
- Natural light and firelight. Dark edges, a warm glow at the centre.
- Natural materials: wood, stone, clay, canvas, hemp, ash.
- Real people doing real things: pouring clay, cooking, practising, harvesting.

**Treatment**
- Keep colour true. Do not tint images purple. The amber of firelight already matches Pumpkin and Amber.
- Frame every image with a 3px Black outline and a hard shadow (Amber on dark surfaces).
- Write alt text that says what is in the picture.

**Source**
- The campfire image looks AI-generated and comes from a phone screenshot. **It is a mood placeholder on the v1 site, not a photo of the property.** Replace it with real photography as soon as possible, and never present generated images as the property.

## 7. Voice (proposal, to be confirmed)

Plain, warm and specific. A knowledgeable host talking to a guest, not a brochure.

**Do**
- Use short sentences and name real things: milk, silk, clay, pine, the river.
- Gloss Sanskrit and Hindi words the first time: *Prakriti* (nature), *Sandhya* (twilight).
- Say what a guest will actually do ("Pour a clay cup", "Sit through a sound healing session").
- Write buttons as the action: "Enquire to stay", "See what we offer".

**Don't**
- Make medical claims. Say the programmes "support wellbeing"; do not say they treat, cure or heal a condition. Keep a short note on the site that programmes do not replace medical care.
- Use stock wellness phrases ("transform your life", "find your best self", "ancient wisdom").
- Use Hindi or Sanskrit as decoration without meaning.
- Promise facilities or figures not yet confirmed (prices, Wi-Fi speed, dates). Use a placeholder chip instead.

## 8. Accessibility

- Every text and background pair on the site comes from the table in 3.5.
- Tap targets are at least 48px.
- Keyboard focus is always visible: 3px Violet outline on ivory, 3px Amber outline on dark purples.
- Headings are in order, landmarks are used, and Devanagari spans carry `lang="hi"`.
- Images have alt text. Decorative stripes and dot rows are `aria-hidden`.
- Motion respects `prefers-reduced-motion`.

```css
  --violet:   #7B2CBF;
  --pumpkin:  #FF6D00;
  --amber:    #FF9E00;

  --font-body:    "Hind", system-ui, sans-serif;
  --font-note:    "Kalam", cursive;

  --line: 3px;                 /* outline weight */
  --shadow-c: var(--black);    /* hard shadow colour: amber or ivory on dark surfaces */
  --offset-s: 4px;             /* hard shadow offsets: box-shadow: X X 0 var(--shadow-c) */
  --offset-m: 6px;
  --offset-l: 10px;
}
```
