Our idea

जड़ों की ओर

Back to the roots.

Shivaya Prakriti is a wellness retreat, a boutique stay and a café in the Himalayas. Healing practice, hands-on making and fresh food sit side by side, among tall trees and beside a river.

Come for a weekend of sound healing and yoga, join a workshop, or stay for weeks and work from the mountains. We buy from the villages around us, grow what we can, and want the area to grow with us.

  • Back to the roots
  • Healing and community
  • Local and sustainable
  • Clean air, trees, river
  • Hands-on creativity

Community and land

समुदाय

Good for the place we are in

गाँव

With the villages

We buy milk, silk, fruit and vegetables from nearby villages, so what we spend stays close to home. We want to help build up the area as part of it.

Plastic that comes in is disposed of properly or recycled. Waste made on site is upcycled where it can be, and some of our workshops are built on that. We grow food here and buy locally.
