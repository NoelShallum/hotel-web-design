    Our idea

    जड़ों की ओर

    Back to the roots.

    Shivaya Prakriti is a small wellness retreat, stay and café in the Himalayas. Healing practice, making things by hand and fresh food sit side by side, among tall trees and beside a river.

    Come for a long weekend away from the city, or stay for weeks and work from the mountains. We buy from the villages around us, grow what we can, and want the area to grow with us.

    • Back to the roots
    • Healing and community
    • Local and sustainable
    • Clean air, trees, river
    • Hands-on creativity

    Community and land

    समुदाय

    Good for the place we are in

    गाँव

    With the villages

    We buy milk, silk, fruit and vegetables from nearby villages, so what we spend stays close to home. We want to help build up the area as part of it.

    धरती

    Light on the land

    Plastic that comes in is disposed of properly or recycled. Waste made on site is upcycled where it can be, and some of our workshops are built on that. We grow food here and buy locally.
