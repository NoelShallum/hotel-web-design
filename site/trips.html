Enquiry first, payment much later

  1. You enquire. Tell us what you have in mind using the form below.
  2. We confirm. The team confirms availability, the itinerary, what is included and the price.
  3. You decide. You decide with the full picture in front of you.
  4. Payment, only then. Nothing is paid before the team has confirmed all of the above. This website does not take payments or confirm bookings.

Before you start

  • The website does not send anything for you. It writes a message for you to read and send in WhatsApp.
  • The team has received nothing until you press send in WhatsApp.
  • Nothing is booked or paid for by enquiring. The team confirms availability, itinerary, included services and price first.
  • Please do not share dates of birth, identity documents or payment details.

When enquiries are unavailable

  • Right now: the business WhatsApp number has not been set, so the form checks your answers and stops there.
  • If JavaScript is turned off in your browser, the form cannot prepare a message.
  • If WhatsApp cannot open on your device, copy the message and paste it into WhatsApp yourself.
